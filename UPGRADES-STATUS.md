# Upgrades: proof-of-liabilities non-omission + proof-of-assets control

Two conceptual upgrades that separate real proof-of-reserves from toy PoR. Both
are implemented in the Soroban contract, exercised by unit tests driven by real
BN254 Groth16 proofs + real ed25519 signatures, and demonstrated on Stellar
testnet with real transactions (every hash re-queryable on Horizon).

- **UPGRADE 1 — proof-of-liabilities NON-OMISSION.** Each customer signs their
  own liability leaf with their ed25519 (Stellar) key. The contract re-verifies
  those signatures on-chain and publishes the signed-leaf registry, so an issuer
  that omits an account to shrink reported liabilities can be caught: the omitted
  user holds a self-authenticating claim the chain accepts while their leaf is
  provably absent from the attested root.
- **UPGRADE 2 — proof-of-assets CONTROL.** The reserve holder must *authorize*
  each attestation (Soroban `require_auth`, a per-invocation ed25519 challenge
  signature). The bound reserve is now a **controlled** account, not merely an
  address that happens to hold tokens.

## UPGRADE 1 — proof-of-liabilities non-omission

### The gap

Every competitor (and the base project through M4) lets the issuer choose which
liabilities to commit. An issuer can **omit** customer accounts entirely to make
`totalLiabilities` look smaller and thus appear solvent with fewer reserves. No
solvency SNARK detects this, because the omitted accounts were simply never in
the tree. This is the completeness half of Vitalik's proof-of-liabilities and the
problem Summa's signed-attestation flow targets.

### The scheme (what we built)

1. **User-signed leaves.** Each customer signs, with their own ed25519 key (the
   curve Stellar accounts use), the canonical 76-byte message

   ```
   "STELLAR-POR-LEAF"(16) || epoch(4 BE) || balance(16 BE i128) || nonce(8 BE) || leaf_commitment(32)
   ```

   where `leaf_commitment = Poseidon(acctCommit, balance)` and
   `acctCommit = Poseidon(pubkeyField, nonce)` binds the user's public key into
   the very leaf the issuer must place in the Merkle-sum tree. The signature
   therefore commits to **which** leaf, **what** balance, and **which** epoch.

2. **On-chain registry with real ed25519 verification.**
   `register_signed_leaves(Vec<SignedLeaf>)` rebuilds each canonical message and
   calls `env.crypto().ed25519_verify` on-chain. It **panics on any invalid
   signature**, so the stored registry can only ever contain leaves genuinely
   signed by their owners for this epoch and balance. The issuer therefore:
   - **cannot fabricate** a leaf for a user it has no signature from, and
   - **cannot downgrade** a user's balance (the signature covers the balance and
     the exact leaf commitment).
   The registry stores the leaf-commitment set + a SHA-256 `registry_root`.

3. **User-side omission detection.** A user confirms two facts:
   - `is_registered(their_leaf_commitment)` — are they on the published board?
   - `verify_inclusion(proof)` — does their leaf sit under the attested
     Merkle-sum root (existing ZK inclusion proof)?
   If the issuer omitted them, at least one is false. Meanwhile
   `verify_signed_claim(epoch, their SignedLeaf)` **still verifies on-chain**,
   returning the exact leaf commitment their signature is bound to. A genuine,
   chain-verified signed claim for a leaf that is absent from the attested root
   is the cryptographic omission proof.

### Circuit impact: none

No circom change was required. The leaf is still a field element; binding the
user's ed25519 key into `acctCommit` happens off-circuit, and the signature is
verified **on-chain** (not in the SNARK). So the existing `solvency` and
`inclusion` circuits and their public-signal layout are unchanged. (The stronger
variant — verifying the signature *inside* the circuit with a circuit-friendly
Baby-Jubjub EdDSA key — is documented under NOT-YET below.)

### Is omission demonstrably detectable? Yes — with one honest caveat

**Detectable and provable: yes.** An omitted user who holds their signed leaf can
show, purely on-chain, that (a) their signature verifies (`verify_signed_claim`
does not panic and returns their leaf commitment) yet (b) their leaf is absent
from the registry (`is_registered=false`) and cannot be proven under the attested
root. That pair is a cryptographic omission proof anyone can re-check.

**The irreducible bulletin-board / vigilance assumption (honest):** the scheme
makes omission *detectable by the victim*, not *unilaterally preventable*. It
requires that (1) each user actually keeps their signed claim and **checks** each
epoch, and (2) the published registry/root is available to users (a bulletin
board). An issuer can still leave a user out of both the tree and the registry;
only that user, by checking, raises the alarm. This is the same completeness
assumption Vitalik and Summa state: full non-omission fundamentally needs users
to participate. We do not claim to remove it — we make the check cheap,
on-chain, and cryptographically conclusive when performed.

## UPGRADE 2 — proof-of-assets control

### The gap

Through M4 the contract bound `reserves` to `balance(reserve_holder, token)` —
strong, but it only proves *some* address holds the tokens. An issuer could point
at a whale's address it does **not** control and claim the balance as backing.

### The scheme (what we built)

`submit_attestation` now calls `config.reserve_holder.require_auth()` before
storing. Soroban `require_auth` forces a per-invocation ed25519 challenge
signature by the reserve holder's key over *(this contract, `submit_attestation`,
these args)* plus a fresh replay-proof nonce. So the bound reserve is a
**controlled** account: an issuer that does not hold the reserve holder's key
cannot produce this authorization and no attestation is stored. The stored
attestation records `control_proven: true`.

### Testnet demonstration

- **Positive (control proven).** The honest `submit_attestation`
  (`eef0e10d…`, above) succeeded only because it was sourced from `zk-reserve`,
  the reserve holder, whose transaction signature satisfied
  `require_auth(reserve_holder)`. Had the holder not signed, the store would not
  have happened.
- **Negative (uncontrolled holder cannot back an attestation).** We deployed a
  second instance `CAMCJIDP2ESXC4PX5EK7I3BG3QZY2DMR6O4PVT7PCI7DEOCEF66ULD2R`
  bound to reserve holder `GDUFSGJVZKAGO4G5XAWYXA5V6BJ2EV62BZKKK445IXA6UHE23WCZEPUW`
  (an account the issuer has no reserve relationship with) and attempted
  `submit_attestation` from `zk-deployer`. It **failed on-chain** with
  `Error(Contract, #13)` — the bound account holds no reserve position for the
  token, so no attestation can be created against it.
- **Rigorous auth-gate proof (unit test).** `submit_without_reserve_holder_auth_fails`
  withholds the reserve holder's authorization (`set_auths(&[])`) and confirms
  `submit_attestation` panics at `require_auth`. This is the authoritative proof
  of the control gate.

**Honest caveat on the testnet negative:** the `stellar` CLI enforces
`require_auth` at *apply* time and auto-signs auth entries for identities in its
local keystore, and simulation does not enforce the auth signature. So the pure
"balance present but holder auth withheld" path is proven cleanly in the unit
test rather than through the CLI; the testnet negative above surfaces at the
cross-contract balance read for an uncontrolled account. Both are real failures;
the unit test is the precise auth-gate evidence.

## Sharpened trust model (precise, layered)

| Property | Guarantee class |
|---|---|
| Every customer balance ≥ 0 (blocks negative-balance / FTX forgery) | **ZK-proven** (in the SNARK) |
| Merkle-sum root + `totalLiabilities` computed correctly | **ZK-proven** |
| `totalLiabilities ≤ reserves` | **ZK-proven** |
| A user's balance is counted under the attested root | **ZK-proven** (inclusion proof) |
| `reserves ≤ balance(reserve_holder, token)` (real, live balance) | **On-chain-bound** (cross-contract read at attestation ledger) |
| The reserve holder **controls** the reserve account | **Signature-proven** (`require_auth`, per-invocation ed25519 challenge) — UPGRADE 2 |
| Each registered leaf was authorized by its owner; issuer cannot fabricate or downgrade a leaf | **Signature-proven** (on-chain `ed25519_verify`) — UPGRADE 1 |
| An omitted user can prove they should have been counted | **Signature-proven + detectable** (weaker tier, on-chain ed25519), conditional on the bulletin-board assumption — UPGRADE 1 (original) |
| Full prevention of omission (root provably contains exactly the registered set) | **✅ ENFORCED ON-CHAIN (FIX 1)** — each leaf's signer key is a PUBLIC input; the contract pins it against the CUSTOMER-self-registered key list (`register_customer_key`, customer-authorized). An issuer cannot author the set, so an attestation missing a registered customer is rejected on-chain (Error #10). Caveat: a malicious single-contributor setup could forge. See `ADVANCED-STATUS.md` FIX 1. |
| In-circuit signature binding (root cryptographically contains only signed leaves) | **✅ DONE** — `circuits/signed_solvency.circom` (~34k constraints, 2^16 ptau, 12 public signals), verified on-chain via `submit_signed_attestation`. |
| Reserves across multiple holders / assets | **✅ MULTI-HOLDER, SAME-UNIT (FIX 2)** — `ReserveLeg` legs + `submit_multi_attestation`, aggregate of live 1:1 balances, control of every leg. Non-1:1 (issuer-set price) is rejected (#13); cross-asset needs a Reflector oracle (NOT-YET). See `ADVANCED-STATUS.md` FIX 2. |
| Portfolio risk limits (concentration cap, min collateralization) | **✅ ZK-PROVEN (per-LEAF, FIX 3)** — `circuits/risk_solvency.circom` + `submit_risk_attestation`; a leaf-concentrated/under-collateralized book is UNPROVABLE. Honest scope: concentration is per-leaf, not per-customer. See `ADVANCED-STATUS.md` FIX 3. |
| Freshness / replay resistance | **✅ DONE (FIX 4)** — `submit_signed_attestation` binds the circuit epoch to a strictly-increasing on-chain counter; a stale proof is rejected (#14). |
| Reserves across other chains | **NOT-YET** (Stellar-only today) |
| Holder = segregated, unencumbered custody | **NOT-YET** (control ≠ segregation) |
| Production trusted setup | **NOT-YET** (Groth16 phase-2 is single-contributor; phase-1 is the public Hermez ptau) |

### Status update — the NOT-YET items below are now DONE (see `ADVANCED-STATUS.md`)

- **In-circuit signature + on-chain key pin (non-omission, FIX 1) — ✅ DONE.** Each
  customer's Baby-JubJub EdDSA signature is verified *inside* the SNARK
  (`circuits/signed_solvency.circom`, ~34k constraints, 2^16 Hermez ptau), and each
  leaf's signer key is a PUBLIC input the contract pins against the CUSTOMER-self-
  registered ordered key list (`register_customer_key`, customer-authorized). The
  issuer never authors the set, so an attestation missing a registered customer is
  rejected on-chain (Error #10). Demonstrated on testnet (honest passes; omission
  rejected #10). Caveat: a malicious single-contributor Groth16 setup could forge.
- **Bulletin-board vigilance — superseded.** The on-chain-ed25519 scheme (retained
  as the weaker tier) needed per-epoch user checks. FIX 1 reduces the assumption to a
  one-time customer self-registration against an issuer-uncontrollable on-chain list.
- **Multi-holder, same-unit reserves — ✅ DONE (FIX 2).** `ReserveLeg` legs +
  `submit_multi_attestation` aggregate live 1:1 balances across multiple controlled
  accounts, requiring control of every leg. Non-1:1 (issuer-set price) is rejected
  (#13). Cross-asset (oracle-priced) and cross-chain remain future work.
- **Portfolio risk limits — ✅ DONE, per-LEAF (FIX 3).** Per-leaf concentration cap +
  min collateralization proven in `circuits/risk_solvency.circom`; a violating book is
  unprovable. Honest scope: concentration is per-leaf, not per-customer (whale-split
  evadable) — per-customer needs FIX 1's keyed leaves merged in (NOT-YET).
- **Freshness / replay — ✅ DONE (FIX 4).** `submit_signed_attestation` binds the
  circuit epoch to a strictly-increasing on-chain counter; a replayed stale proof is
  rejected (#14).
- **Trusted setup — still NOT-YET.** Single-contributor phase-2; a production
  deployment needs a multi-party ceremony (see `circuits/README.md`).
